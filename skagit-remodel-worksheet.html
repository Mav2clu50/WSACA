    Methodology. Weighted effective age per IAAO, Property Assessment Valuation (3rd ed.): the effective age of a modernized, rehabilitated, or remodeled improvement is computed from construction components, weighting the renewed share of replacement cost at the age of the remodel and the untouched share at its prior effective age. Additions are blended by share of total cost at their own age, modernized portions computed first. Quality reflects materials and workmanship of the whole dwelling; suggestions here always require field confirmation.
