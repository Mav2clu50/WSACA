A growing collection of open-source tools developed by the Washington State Association of County Assessors. Modern infrastructure should be a shared resource, not something every county office has to build independently.

About

The Washington State Association of County Assessors represents all 39 county assessor offices in Washington. Most counties operate with limited technical staff, and most of the digital work involved in property tax administration would benefit from being built once, shared, and maintained collectively.

Some of the tools below serve homeowners directly; others are built for assessor staff to use during the assessment cycle. All are free, open source, and run entirely in the browser. No login, no data collection, no installation — free for any county office to link to or embed.

Tools

Each tool is purpose-built for a specific audience, free to use, and self-contained — no installation, no backend, no data leaving the browser.

For homeowners

Property Tax Exemption Screening Tool

Washington's property tax exemption program for senior citizens and people with disabilities expanded substantially in tax year 2027 under Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill 6162. This screening tool walks a homeowner from county selection through eligibility, household composition, and an income calculation, then returns a clear, county-specific result with a printable summary.

  • Walks homeowners through 5 questions: county, age or disability, residence, household, income
  • Returns a clear A/B/C level result showing what they'd be exempt from
  • Compares 2026 thresholds to the new 2027 law for the county they select
  • Generates a printable summary with the documents to bring to the assessor's office
  • Links directly to the county assessor's office for the official determination

For assessor offices

Sales Ratio Analysis

A self-contained sales ratio analysis tool for assessor offices to use during the assessment cycle, before certified values are finalized. Upload a validated sales file and produce IAAO 2025-grounded ratio statistics, vertical equity diagnostics, and modifier recommendations. Not a substitute for the official vertical equity report.

  • Accepts CSV or Excel (.xlsx) sales files with automatic column mapping
  • Overall ratio diagnostics: median, COD, PRD, PRB with IAAO threshold flags
  • Time adjustment, vertical equity, and stratified analysis by neighborhood or use code
  • Modifier recommendations to support assessment cycle decisions
  • Runs entirely in the browser; no data leaves the analyst's machine
