About

Washington's property tax exemption program for senior citizens and people with disabilities expanded substantially in tax year 2027 under Engrossed Substitute Senate Bill 6162. New thresholds. New deductions. A consolidated state school levy. Larger benefits at every qualification level.

WSACA built this screening tool so that any of Washington's 39 county assessor offices can hand a homeowner a clear, county-specific answer to the question: do I qualify, and what would I save? No login, no data collection, no installation. The tool runs in any browser, points the applicant toward their county assessor for the official determination, and is free for any county office to link to or embed.

The recommended version

A guided screening tool that helps homeowners check whether they may qualify for the expanded 2027 property tax exemption program in their county. Walks the user from county selection through eligibility, household composition, and an income calculation, then returns a clear, county-specific result with a printable summary.

  • Walks homeowners through 5 questions: county, age or disability, residence, household, income
  • Returns a clear A/B/C level result showing what they'd be exempt from
  • Compares 2026 thresholds to the new 2027 law for the county they select
  • Generates a printable summary with the documents to bring to the assessor's office
  • Links directly to the county assessor's office for the official determination
